overview: >
  The Silva Residence concept creates a quiet, contemporary home through the deliberate use of limestone, deep horizontal overhangs, and intimate garden courts. The design prioritizes framed views, natural light, and a serene material palette that responds to the Jordanian context.

  Horizontal roof planes cast deep shadows across the facade, creating a play of light and shadow that shifts throughout the day. Garden courts are positioned at key vantage points, offering curated views of mature planting and water features.

  The material palette is restrained: honed limestone for structure and cladding, timber for warmth, and glass for transparency. Every material choice is driven by the desire to create a home that feels both contemporary and grounded.
designApproach: >
  The residence is organized around a series of garden courts that serve as spatial anchors. Each court is framed by the building mass and offers a distinct experience — from the entry court of welcome to the private garden of the master suite.

  Deep overhangs are the defining architectural feature. They provide shade, create dramatic shadow lines, and blur the boundary between interior and exterior. The facade reads as a composition of solid and void, mass and void, light and shadow.
