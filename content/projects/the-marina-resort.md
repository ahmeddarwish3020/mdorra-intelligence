overview: >
  The Marina Resort concept is designed as a coastal destination where architecture, landscape, and water merge seamlessly. The strong indoor–outdoor program creates a fluid experience between interior spaces and the seaside environment.

  A panoramic pool deck extends the living space toward the sea, framed by palm-lined landscaping and warm evening lighting that creates a relaxed destination atmosphere. Guest rooms open directly onto terraces with uninterrupted views of the coastline.

  The material palette reflects the coastal context: light limestone, natural timber, and woven textiles. Water features are integrated throughout the property, creating a continuous sensory connection to the sea.
designApproach: >
  The resort is conceived as a sequence of outdoor rooms punctuated by architectural moments — reception pavilions, restaurant terraces, pool edges, and lounging platforms. Each space is designed to frame the sea and landscape as a living backdrop.

  The indoor–outdoor program is the core design strategy. Sliding glass walls, open-air pavilions, and terrace transitions dissolve the boundary between built environment and natural landscape.
