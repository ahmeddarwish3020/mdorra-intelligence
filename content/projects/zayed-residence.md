  A penthouse interior that balances openness with intimacy, using a restrained material palette to frame panoramic Gulf views. The design transforms a generic developer shell into a calibrated domestic landscape.
overview: >
  The Zayed Residence occupies the top floor of a waterfront tower in Dubai Marina. The client, a young family, wanted a home that felt spacious and light-filled while providing acoustic separation between public and private zones. The existing layout was a typical open-plan developer configuration with little consideration for privacy or material continuity.

  The intervention reorganizes the plan around a central spine — a floor-to-ceiling walnut volume that conceals storage, AV, and mechanical systems while defining circulation. To the north, the living-dining-kitchen sequence opens fully to the marina panorama through minimal sliding glazing. To the south, bedrooms and family spaces are buffered by the central volume, ensuring acoustic privacy.

  Materiality is deliberately limited: European walnut (joinery, wall panels, doors), honed Crema Marfil limestone (floors, wet areas), white oak (custom furniture), and brushed brass (hardware, lighting details). The palette reads as a single gesture — warm, tactile, and timeless. All joinery is handled as architecture: flush doors, invisible hinges, integrated handles, recessed lighting within panel reveals.
designApproach: >
  The design strategy is subtraction over addition. Rather than layering decoration onto the developer shell, we removed dropped ceilings, bulkheads, and standard finishes to reveal the concrete soffit (3.4 m clear height). The central walnut volume is the only inserted element — everything else is alignment, lighting, and material continuity.

  Lighting is zoned by activity, not by room: perimeter slots wash walls, adjustable downlights target art and surfaces, decorative pendants mark the dining table and kitchen island. All circuits are dimmable, controlled via a single keypad per zone — no apps, no complexity.

  The kitchen is conceived as a piece of furniture, not a fitted unit. Walnut base cabinets, limestone countertop and backsplash, integrated Gaggenau appliances. The island doubles as a breakfast bar and prep station, with a concealed pop-up extractor.

  In the master suite, the bed floats on a raised walnut platform with integrated nightstands and indirect lighting. The walk-in closet is lined in the same walnut, with brass hanging rails and leather-lined drawers. The en-suite bathroom continues the limestone floor into the shower zone (linear drain, frameless glass), with a freestanding stone bathtub positioned for the view.
