  A refined residential interior concept combining warm oak, natural stone, bronze detailing, and panoramic city views. The layout prioritizes calm circulation, layered lighting, and generous entertaining spaces.
overview: >
  The Cove Residence concept explores how material warmth and spatial calm can coexist in an urban context. The design centers on a central spine of walnut joinery that defines circulation while concealing storage and mechanical systems.

  To the north, living spaces open fully to panoramic city views through minimal sliding glazing. To the south, private zones are buffered by the central volume, ensuring acoustic privacy.

  Materiality is deliberately limited: European walnut, honed Crema Marfil limestone, white oak, and brushed brass. The palette reads as a single gesture — warm, tactile, and timeless. All joinery is handled as architecture.
designApproach: >
  The design strategy is subtraction over addition. Rather than layering decoration, we removed dropped ceilings and standard finishes to reveal clean ceiling heights. The central walnut volume is the only inserted element — everything else is alignment, lighting, and material continuity.

  Lighting is zoned by activity, not by room: perimeter slots wash walls, adjustable downlights target art and surfaces, decorative pendants mark the dining table and kitchen island. All circuits are dimmable and controlled via a single keypad per zone.
