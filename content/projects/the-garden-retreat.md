overview: >
  The Garden Retreat concept transforms a private estate into a sequence of outdoor rooms for relaxation and entertaining. The design weaves mature planting, reflecting water, and warm architectural lighting through a carefully choreographed landscape journey.

  Each outdoor room serves a distinct purpose — from the entry garden of welcome to the dining terrace framed by mature trees, to the reflection pool that mirrors the sky. The landscape is designed to be experienced sequentially, with each space revealing a new perspective.

  Architectural elements — stone walls, timber pergolas, and lighting installations — are integrated into the planting scheme rather than imposed upon it. The result is a landscape that feels both wild and meticulously composed.
designApproach: >
  The retreat is designed as a journey through outdoor rooms, each with its own character and purpose. The landscape architecture creates a sequence of experiences that move from formal entry to wild garden, from intimate courtyard to expansive terrace.

  Mature planting is the foundation. Existing trees are preserved and enhanced, while new plantings are selected for seasonal interest, texture, and fragrance. Water features — reflecting pools, gentle streams, and a central fountain — provide acoustic and visual calm.

  Architectural lighting is designed to extend the landscape into the evening. Warm-toned fixtures highlight planting, illuminate pathways, and create focal points that transform the retreat after dark.
